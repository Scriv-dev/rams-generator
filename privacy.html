Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This notice explains what information the RAMS Generator website collects, why, and who handles it. In short: what you type into the generator stays on your device. We only receive feedback you choose to send us and anonymous visit counts.

What you type into the generator

Event details, staff names and generated documents are processed in your browser. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

To save you retyping, the generator keeps your form entries and any RAMS you save in your browser's local storage on your device. You can delete them at any time with Clear form, the Delete button in Saved RAMS, or by clearing your browser data for this site.

Feedback you send us

If you use Send feedback, we receive:

  • your message and the topic you picked, if any
  • your email address, only if you enter one (so we can reply)
  • the page you sent it from
  • from the generator: the event type, the activities ticked and the detail level chosen

We never receive event names, venues, clients or staff names through the feedback form.

Why: to understand what's missing and improve the tool, and to reply if you asked us to. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in improving the service, and your consent where you give an email address for a reply.

Who handles it: feedback is delivered to us by email through Formspree, a form service. Formspree may process it outside the UK; it says it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers.

How long: we keep feedback for as long as we need it to act on it, and no longer than two years.

Counting visits

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites.

For each page view, this site sends GoatCounter the page address (without anything after a ? or #), the page title, the website that linked you here, and your screen width. We also count a few anonymous actions: generating, saving and printing a RAMS, sending feedback, and clicking to upgrade. None of these include anything you typed.

Like any website request, GoatCounter's server sees your IP address and browser details when the count is sent. GoatCounter says it doesn't store these: it uses them briefly to tell visits apart and keeps only totals, such as visits per page, browser type and country.

You can opt out: if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, this site doesn't count your visit at all.

Why: to see how many people use the tool and which features they use. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in understanding use of the service.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the technical information your browser sends with every request, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies. The local storage described above stays on your device, is used only to keep your own work, and isn't used for tracking.

Accounts and payments

Accounts and paid plans aren't available yet. We'll update this notice before they launch.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. To do this, use Send feedback and include an email address so we can reply. In practice, the only personal information we may hold is feedback you sent us.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

If we change how the site handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
