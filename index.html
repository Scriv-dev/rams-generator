Who it's for

Anyone in the UK who has to hand a venue, client or safety officer a RAMS before they're allowed on site.

  • Event and production companies

    Festivals, corporate events, weddings and outdoor shows, with a consistent RAMS for every job.

  • Site contractors

    Staging, marquee, fencing, power and rigging crews who need method statements for their part of the build.

  • Small teams and freelancers

    Crew chiefs and production managers without a health and safety department to write documents for them.

  • Venue and event managers

    A clear structure to compare against the RAMS suppliers send you, or a starting point for your own.

How it works

  1. Enter the event

    Event name, date, venue, client and type: festival, corporate, wedding or outdoor show.

  2. Tick the activities

    Choose what's happening on site, from power distribution to vehicle movements.

  3. Check and edit

    Read every section. Tap any text to change it and tap a rating to adjust it.

  4. Save and print

    Save it in your browser to reopen later, then print it or save it as a PDF for sign-off.

What's in the document

  • Scope of the work, the event and the legislation referenced
  • Risk assessments for each activity: hazard, who might be harmed, existing controls, low / medium / high rating and further actions
  • Method statement with numbered steps for setup, during the event and pack-down
  • PPE list matched to the activities you ticked
  • Wind action plan for outdoor structures, with amber and red actions
  • Emergency arrangements to fill in for the site
  • Sign-off sheet for crew names and signatures, plus approval

Written for UK sites

Controls are specific, not generic: 30 mA RCDs, IP44 CEE-form connectors, ballasted fence stays every 4th panel, banksman rules, and MEWP wind limits read at platform height.

Where your data is kept

Without an account, everything you type and every RAMS you save stays in the browser you're using. Nothing is uploaded, so saved RAMS don't sync between devices, and clearing your browser data deletes them.

With Pro, the RAMS you save are stored in your account so you can open them anywhere you sign in. Only you can see them. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card details.

Visitor numbers are counted without cookies and without collecting personal data. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, you aren't counted at all. Read the privacy notice.

Disclaimer

Every RAMS this tool produces is a draft. It is a starting point based on common UK event practice, not a finished safety document.

Before work starts, a competent person must check it, adapt it to the specific site, venue, contractors and event, and approve it. Wind speeds, load ratings and other figures must come from your own suppliers' design calculations and manuals.

Using this tool does not replace your duty to carry out a suitable and sufficient risk assessment under the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999. The authors accept no liability for how generated documents are used.
